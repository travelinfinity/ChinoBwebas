Sample Blueprint: The 8-Minute Station Pacing Formula

From Chapter 1 of the AHPRA RN OSCE 10 Station Simplified Guide (First Edition)

Minutes 0:00 – 1:30 | Safe Entry & 3-Point ID

Check scene safety, perform Bare Below the Elbows Hand Hygiene, introduce yourself with role, close privacy curtain, verify 3 patient identifiers (Name, DOB, URN) verbally and on wristband, verify Allergies, explain procedure, and gain verbal consent.

Minutes 1:30 – 5:30 | Core Clinical Task & Assessment

Execute the station's primary clinical intervention (e.g., A-G Assessment, ANTT Wound Dressing, IV/Oral Medication Administration, or Physiological Observations) while maintaining ongoing therapeutic communication and pain checks.

Minutes 5:30 – 7:00 | Real-Time Australian Charting

Document findings accurately in black/blue ink on the ADDS, NIMC, or Fluid Balance chart. Calculate total ADDS/EWS score, print name, sign, and record date/time.

Minutes 7:00 – 8:00 | Safe Closure & Escalation/Handover

Ensure bed is in lowest position, brakes locked, call bell within reach, sharps/waste disposed of correctly, perform final hand hygiene, and deliver crisp ISBAR escalation if clinical triggers are met.
